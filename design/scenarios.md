# Scenarios

## 1. Evaluate Babysitter Response to a Crisis

The AI agent receives a babysitter's text response to a stressful childcare scenario and evaluates her patience level and primary educational approach.

| Babysitter Response | Patience Level | Approach |
|---------------------|----------------|----------|
| I would take a deep breath, pick the baby up, and sing a quiet song until he calms down. | High | Comforting |
| I'd put him in his crib and leave the room for 5 minutes so he learns to self-soothe. | Low | Strict / Independent |
| I will try to distract him with a noisy toy or give him a screen to watch. | Medium | Distraction |
